# Brief

The participants are headless models. They see this brief, the context files
and folders you pass, and the web. They do not see the user's memory, chat
history or connected apps. Write everything they need here.

## Problem
What needs deciding or producing, in one or two sentences.

## Why it matters and what good looks like
The outcome the user wants and how they will judge the answer.

## Background
Facts about the user's situation that change the answer: role, tools already
in use, past attempts, people involved, location, timing.

## Constraints
Budget, time, technology, policy, things that must or must not happen.

## Already decided
Choices that are not up for debate, with the reason.

## Out of scope
What the participants should not spend time on.

## Context provided
Files and folders passed with --context, and what each one is for.

## Output wanted
The form of the final proposal: a recommendation, a plan, a comparison, a
draft. Length and audience.
